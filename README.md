## ⚡ About Me

I'm a dedicated **Software Engineering student** currently pursuing my **Higher National Diploma (HND) at NIBM**.

My interests sit at the intersection of **software engineering, full-stack development, database architecture, system administration, UI/UX design, and emerging AI technologies**.

I enjoy turning complex technical problems into software that is not only functional and scalable, but also intuitive and visually engaging.
